About this page

Made by LT Sean Egan and AG1 Derek Conklin for our OCEANO and AG teammates.

The FNMOC Sub Support page lists each NOSSP wavelet area by name with its corners in latitude and longitude, and nothing shows where those corners fall. This page draws every area as a box on a map. Hover a box to name it, click it to see the lat/lon, resolution and model, and the forecast-hour links that download the wavelet straight from the portal.

The area list is a snapshot of the FNMOC page. Boxes and links change rarely; the date and hours on the portal are the ones that count. Source and a refresh script: github.com/sdegan-USN/wavelet-map.
